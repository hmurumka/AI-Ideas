# 🤖 Agentic Product Manager AI Workflow

## 1. Requirement Gathering
- **Inputs:** Customer feedback, Sales updates, Market context.
- **AI Role:**
  - Centralized intake portal for asynchronous updates.
  - Auto-classify into:
    - **Known work** → Directly mapped to repos/components.
    - **Unknown work** → Flagged as JIRA Spikes (POC, research).

---

## 2. Impact Analysis
- AI scans codebase & services context.
- Identifies impacted repos/components.
- Generates **dependency graph** (component-wise).
- Highlights **cross-team dependencies**.

---

## 3. Epic & Story Creation
- **Business Epic** → Quarterly roadmap chunks.
- **Technical Epics** → Component-level breakdown.
- **Stories** → Known tasks, ready for implementation.
- **Spikes** → Unknowns requiring exploration.

---

## 4. Approval Workflow
- Every story starts as **Ready for Approval**.
- AI generates **approval checklist** (Architect, QA Lead, Product Owner).
- Agents remain locked until all approvals are ✅.
- Once approved:
  - **Epic Agent** orchestrates sub-agents:
    - Coding Agent → Implements repo changes.
    - Testing Agent → Builds regression/unit tests.
    - Documentation Agent → Updates Confluence.

---

## 5. Execution Flow
```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Story Created] --> B[Approval Pending]
    B -->|All Approved| C[Epic Agent Orchestrates]
    C --> D[Coding Agent]
    C --> E[Testing Agent]
    C --> F[Documentation Agent]
    D --> G[Demo/Review]
    E --> G
    F --> G
    G --> H[Closed + Audit Trail]

## 6. Documentation Automation
  - Confluence Pages auto-generated:
    - Business Epic overview.
    - HLD (High-Level Design) diagrams.
    - LLD (Low-Level Design) component changes.
    - Dependency graphs.

7. Governance & Approvals
  - At each milestone:
    - AI generates approval checklist.
    - Tracks demos & reviews.
    - Logs decisions for audit trail.

8. Weekly Async Sync
  - AI prepares summary dashboards:
    - Approved vs pending stories.
    - Execution progress by sub-agents.
    - Risks & blockers.

Shared in async meeting → replaces daily communication.
