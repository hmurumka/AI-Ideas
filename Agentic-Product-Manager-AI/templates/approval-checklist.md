# Approval Checklist Template

Default approvers:
- Architect
- QA Lead
- Product Owner

Checklist items:
- [ ] Architecture review completed (Architect)
- [ ] Tests planned and coverage targets met (QA Lead)
- [ ] Business acceptance criteria validated (Product Owner)
- [ ] Security and compliance review completed (Security)
- [ ] Deployment plan documented (DevOps/Release)

Procedure:
- AI sends approval notification (JIRA/Slack/Teams) with checklist link.
- Record approver name and timestamp when they mark their item.
- Only when all required items are checked does the story move to In Progress and agents can run.
